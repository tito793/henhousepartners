Leadership Across Our Ventures

TH

Tito Hen

Founder & Executive Chairman

A senior technology executive and serial entrepreneur with over two decades of experience scaling businesses across Japan and the US. As the Founder of KeyTalent Inc. and HenHouseTechnology, and Co-Founder of HenHouse Japan and Academate.com, he combines enterprise sales discipline with executive search expertise to build high-growth, venture-backed tech platforms and talent solutions across APAC.

  • Ventures & Strategy: Founded Tokyo’s premier tech search firm, KeyTalent Inc., specializing in Software, SaaS, Cloud, AI, and Cybersecurity. Co-founded HenHouse Japan and Academate.com to drive regional expansion and digital learning.
  • Recruitment Excellence: Over a decade as a top-billing Partner and Practice Leader across Japan & APAC, earning multiple #1 recruiter awards from major enterprise software vendors and leading multi-P&L divisions.
  • Enterprise Sales: Silicon Valley and Tokyo enterprise sales background with Oracle, Siebel Systems (Global Excellence Award winner), and Walker Interactive Systems.
AS

Aki Serizawa

Chief Operating Officer

Aki Serizawa brings technology, creative storytelling, and operational experience to HenHouse Partners, helping grow HenHouse Japan’s food import business, managing Academate.co.jp, and supporting KeyTalent Inc.

  • Food Business & Creative Direction: Her creative work for the HEN HOUSE Rakuten shop introduces Cambodian spices, including Kampot pepper, through visual storytelling and clear product information for Japanese customers.
  • Operations & Education: KeyTalent’s Office Manager / Engagement Manager since September 2022, coordinating workflows, schedules, communications, records, and client support alongside Academate management.
  • Documentary & Editorial Experience: Media Director from 2006 to 2022, creating acclaimed Japanese documentaries through editorial vision, narrative structure, and authentic storytelling.
  • Technology & Project Delivery: Managed IT translation projects at Adept (2003–2006), coordinating delivery and quality. Earlier, as a Systems Engineer at TISI (1999–2003), supported financial ERP implementation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and optimization.

A Waseda University graduate in Political Science and Economics, she is bilingual in Japanese and English.
